MissNothing

Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

MissNothing (“the app”) is a local alarm layer over school circulars. This policy describes how the app uses Gmail on your device, and what a Google Cloud token broker may store. It is written for Google’s OAuth consent screen and for you.

Who we are

The app is developed by Vinodh Nagarajaiah. There is no MissNothing user account and no password. Questions: open an issue on the GitHub repository.

What the app accesses

When you tap Connect Gmail, the app requests the Google scope gmail.readonly only. It never requests send, compose, or modify scopes. The app can read mail you already have in Gmail; it cannot send mail as you.

The app uses that access to:

  • list recent message headers so you can allowlist school senders
  • download bodies of messages from senders you allowlisted
  • extract proposed actions (dress, bring, dates, opt-in offers) on the device
  • schedule local notifications on this phone

Where mail is stored

Mail the app downloads is stored only on this device, in a local encrypted database. Google already stores your Gmail; this app does not create a second cloud copy of message bodies, attachments, parsed cards, or events.

A Google Cloud backend may exist to refresh OAuth access, renew Gmail mailbox watches, and send a wake-up ping to this phone. That backend stores only a KMS-encrypted refresh token and watch metadata. It must never store mail bodies, attachments, proposals, or events.

If a language model runs, it runs on the device (or as an OS system model). Mail is not sent to a cloud AI API.

What we do not collect

  • no analytics, crash reporters, or advertising identifiers that leave the phone
  • no address book scrape beyond the Gmail headers of recent mail, used to suggest an allowlist
  • no sale or sharing of mail with third parties, because we never receive it

Notifications

Event reminders are local notifications scheduled by the app on this device after you add a card. A Cloud Messaging ping may wake the app to sync; that ping does not contain mail content.

Your choices

  • Disconnect Gmail in the app, or revoke access at Google Account permissions.
  • Uninstall the app to delete the on-device copy.
  • If you lose the phone and did not export a backup, that copy is gone. That is by design.

Children

The app is for a parent’s own Gmail. It is not directed at children and does not create child accounts.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change. The URL of this page is what Google’s consent screen uses; it may later move to missnothing.app with the same content.
