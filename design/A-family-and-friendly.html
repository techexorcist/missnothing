Every screen below uses the two real circulars as content, so nothing here is lorem ipsum. Where a screen shows a date it is 19 August 2026 — the day the parser had to get right.

Design thesis. Friendly surfaces, urgent signals. Warmth lives in the shapes, the palette and the copy; it never leaks into the alarm. The act today badge and the need-by line stay the loudest things on screen, because a cute app that whispers is the failure this product exists to prevent. No illustrations — a solo build cannot maintain an illustration set, so friendliness comes from radius, spacing and voice instead.

Five rules the visuals enforce

  • The item is the headline. Never a festival name, never "Circular 34". A card whose title is "Independence Day celebration" has failed even if every field is correct.
  • Friendly surface, urgent signal. Rounded, warm, human copy — but act-today and the need-by line are the highest-contrast elements on the screen.
  • Nothing hides. Nothing-found and sync-incomplete are destinations with counts, not silence. The app is a claim; the UI has to be able to admit a miss.
  • Sanitise at render only. Stored text is the school's exact words; URLs, phone numbers and bidi marks are stripped when drawn. A crafted mail can never become "School: call +91… urgently" on a lock screen.
  • Type never signalled by colour alone. Left edge plus a worded chip, so it still reads in greyscale.

Open questions for you

  • Is terracotta right, or too close to a food-delivery app? An alternative is deep teal with the same warm canvas.
  • Should the tab bar exist in v1 at all, or is Review the whole app until there are enough confirmed items for Agenda to be worth a tab?
  • Item checkboxes: useful ("I've packed the snacks bag"), or clutter on a card you only see once?
  • Should "Maybe" appear on dated and undated cards too, or only on decisions?
